3. [CareerHub — Product & Profile Fleet](https://github.com/orgs/Motherpher/projects/4) — organisation Project, 13 native engine/UX/search/HRDM/release Issues; no personal profile records copied.

A fourth domain board is **NO-GO unless a new distinct operating need is evidenced and owner approves**. FFC and FFOS retain their existing formal estate associations.

All 31 repositories retain GitHub Projects feature flags as source metadata; feature enabled does not mean a board exists. The three new boards are private and are **not** substitutes for the estate-level Project or original Issues.

## Authority, privacy and operations boundaries

- The original source Issues are not duplicated, closed, relabelled or silently accepted. The same native Issue may be represented in its dedicated domain board and in the private Estate Portfolio without copying it.
- Adding a board does **not** grant new access to a private repository, prove publishing rights or create a production application.
- Default unsafe **Auto-close issue** workflows were deleted from the three new boards; automatic sub-Issue intake was also removed on Opal and Politent. No source Issue status was changed.
- Additional boards are NOT required for historical Tier D and selectively governed Tier C projects; the Director may request separate future evidence and scoped approval.
- `Motherpher/FFOS` and `Motherpher/form-flode` maintain separate canonical responsibilities. No FFC Corpus version or CDX policy is changed by this decision.
- The account's other empty historical boards (Motherpher #1 closed; Hybrismannen Soundsystem #1 open) remain unchanged; no deletion/archive decision inferred.
